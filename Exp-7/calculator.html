7.9 Analysis and Calculations:

Calculation for \(X\):

\( X = \frac{Rl}{100 - l} \) (Left gap)
\( X = \frac{R(100 - l)}{l} \) (Right gap)

Calculate mean \(X\) = \(\frac{X_1+X_2+X_3+X_4}{4}\) = - \(\Omega\)

Calculation for specific resistance:

Specific resistance of the material of the given wire,

\( \rho = \frac{X\pi r^2}{L} \) = - \(\Omega \cdot cm\)

Standard value of the specific resistance, \(\rho_0\) = \(\Omega \cdot cm\)

Percentage error = \( \frac{|\rho - \rho_0|}{\rho_0} \times 100 \% \) = - %
